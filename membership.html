FAQ

Common questions.

Can I cancel my membership at any time?
Yes. Professional memberships can be cancelled at any time. Annual plans are non-refundable after the first 30 days, but you retain access until the end of your billing period.
What's included in the premium content library?
The library includes all DAS-published articles, deep dives, case studies, research reports, and recordings from past events. New content is added regularly by community contributors.
How does the mentorship programme work?
Professional members can apply for the mentorship programme each quarter. You'll be matched with a mentor based on your goals, role, and area of focus for monthly one-to-one sessions.
How does the Corporate plan work?
Corporate plans are custom-scoped to your organisation's size and needs. Contact us to discuss seat volumes, event sponsorship, custom onboarding, and publishing benefits for your team.
Is there a student or early-career discount?
Yes. Students and professionals within two years of starting their career can access a reduced rate. Contact us with your details to receive a discount code before purchasing.
