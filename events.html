Web Analytics Wednesday

Our flagship monthly gathering, Web Analytics Wednesday (WAW), is where practitioners come to share what actually works. No vendor pitches. No polished case studies. Just honest conversations about data.

Started by Steen Rasmussen, WAW has grown from a room of 30 colleagues to a global series of 400+ practitioners sharing knowledge across cities and formats.
